Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CashLens

Is CashLens free to use?

Yes! CashLens is completely free to download and use all features. We plan to add premium features in the future, but all current functionality will remain free.

Do I need to create an account?

No. CashLens works completely offline with no account required. Just download and start tracking.

Where is my data stored?

All your financial data is stored locally on your device only. We do not store your financial information in the cloud or on our servers.

Can I export my data?

Yes, you can export your expense data as CSV or JSON files for use in spreadsheets or other financial tools.

Is my financial data secure?

Yes. Since your data never leaves your device (unless you choose to export it), it's protected by your device's built-in security measures like Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode.

How do I create a custom category?

Tap the "Manage" button in the category selector when adding an expense, then tap "Add New Category" to create a custom category with your choice of name, icon, and color.
