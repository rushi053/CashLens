Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2025

Introduction

At CashLens, we take your privacy seriously. This policy describes what information our app collects and how it's used.

Information Collection and Use

CashLens is designed with privacy as a core principle. We collect no personal information about you or your finances. All data you enter into the app (expenses, categories, etc.) remains solely on your device.

Data Storage

All your financial data is stored locally on your device using secure iOS storage systems. We do not use cloud storage for your financial information, and your data is never transmitted to our servers or third parties.

Analytics

We use limited, anonymized analytics to improve the app experience. This includes:

  • App performance metrics
  • Feature usage statistics
  • Crash reports

All analytics data is anonymized and contains no personally identifiable information or details about your expenses.

Third-Party Services

CashLens uses Apple's App Store services for distribution. Please refer to Apple's privacy policy for information about their data practices.

Your Rights

Since we don't collect your personal data, there's nothing for us to delete, correct, or export. Your data belongs to you and stays on your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the app.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@cashlens.app.
